How it works

Sensor readings flow through four stages, and the last one is what a maintenance engineer sees.

  1. Collect

    A virtual IoT simulator streams readings every 10 minutes from 40 machines that wear out and fail, with realistic noise and missing values.

  2. Clean and enrich

    Gaps are filled per machine, then rolling averages and trends reveal slow wear that single readings hide.

  3. Predict

    Logistic Regression and Random Forest models estimate the chance of failure in the next 8 hours.

  4. Alert

    Above 50% the system raises a warning. Above 80% it marks the machine critical.

Results on machines the model never saw

Training and testing use different machines, so the scores are not inflated by memorizing a machine's history.

Recall is the number to watch: a missed failure costs far more than a false alarm.

The failure probability stays near zero, then climbs hours before the true failure zone.

The data is simulated, so real plants will score lower. The pipeline accepts real datasets such as NASA C-MAPSS.
